Frequently asked

I bought "No ads" / coins but don't see them. Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple and linked to your Apple ID.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes the progress.

Does it need internet? No. Only ads and purchases use the network.
