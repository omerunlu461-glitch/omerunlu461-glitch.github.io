Rescue Town: Merge & Save

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Summary

Rescue Town does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your game progress

Your progress (items, coins, gems, town and settings) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was completed, so it can deliver the item. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Analytics, crash reporting and device statistics are turned off in this game. We do not use advertising networks or any other third-party SDK that collects data.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: omerunlu461@gmail.com
