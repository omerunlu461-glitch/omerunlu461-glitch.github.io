Rescue Town: Merge & Save

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary

Rescue Town has no accounts and no analytics, and we (the developer) do not collect any personal data. The only third-party service that may process data is Google AdMob, and only for optional reward videos that you choose to watch. Game Center is provided by Apple.

Your game progress

Your progress (items, coins, gems, town and settings) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was completed, so it can deliver the item. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Optional reward videos (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video to get extra energy or gems (at most 4 per day). There are no banner or pop-up ads. These videos are served by Google AdMob. Before any video loads, the game shows Google's consent form where required and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

To show and measure ads, Google may process: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), an approximate location based on your IP address, information about the ads you see and tap, and basic diagnostic data. If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Game Center and sharing

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores (people rescued, mayor level) and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center. The Share button creates a picture of your town on your device; it is only sent where you choose to share it.

Other

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Analytics, crash reporting and device statistics are turned off. Reminders are local notifications created on your device. We do not use any other third-party SDK that collects data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Reward videos are optional.
