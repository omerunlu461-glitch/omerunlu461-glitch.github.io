Frequently asked

How do I play? Drag two identical items together to merge them into a better one. Tap a toolbox or supply crate to get new items. Give people what they need to rescue them and earn stars.

Why are there no people on the street? Between disasters, rebuild buildings in Town with your stars. The next disaster and its people arrive when enough of the town is rebuilt.

I bought something but don't see it. Tap the gem counter at the top to open the Shop, then tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple and linked to your Apple ID.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes the progress.
